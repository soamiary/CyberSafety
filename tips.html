Cyber Safety Tips

Simple habits that make a huge difference.

Staying safe online doesn't require advanced technical skills. Following a few good habits every day can protect your personal information from hackers, scams and identity theft.

Why Cyber Safety Matters

Every day millions of people browse the internet, shop online, communicate through social media and store personal information on digital devices. Cyber criminals constantly search for vulnerable users, making cyber safety an essential life skill.

By following a few simple precautions, you can greatly reduce the chances of becoming a victim of cybercrime.

10 Essential Cyber Safety Tips

Create Strong Passwords

Use long passwords with uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Never reuse passwords across multiple websites.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Adding an extra verification step makes your accounts much harder to hack.

Keep Software Updated

Updates fix security vulnerabilities that hackers often exploit.

Think Before Clicking

Avoid suspicious emails, attachments and unknown links.

Use Secure Wi-Fi

Avoid entering passwords or banking information while connected to public Wi-Fi.

Backup Important Files

Store copies of important documents in secure cloud storage or external drives.

Protect Personal Information

Never overshare sensitive information on social media or public websites.

Lock Your Devices

Use Face ID, Fingerprint or PIN protection on every device.

Install Antivirus Software

Reliable antivirus software helps detect and block malicious programs.

Daily Cyber Safety Checklist

  • Update your software regularly.
  • Verify links before clicking.
  • Lock your devices when away.
  • Use strong unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Back up important files.
  • Review your privacy settings.

Do's and Don'ts

DO

  • Create strong passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Verify website addresses.
  • Use antivirus software.
  • Report suspicious emails.

DON'T

  • Reuse passwords.
  • Share OTP codes.
  • Click unknown links.
  • Download random files.
  • Ignore security updates.
  • Connect to unsafe Wi-Fi.

Protect Yourself Before It's Too Late

Cybersecurity begins with small habits that you practice every day. Stay alert and stay protected.

Why we chose to talk about CyberSafety?

Our lives are more connected than ever. We rely on the internet for communication, education, banking, shopping, and entertainment. However, this increased digital presence also creates more opportunities for cybercriminals to exploit weaknesses and target individuals, businesses, and organizations.

Cyberattacks are becoming more frequent and sophisticated, with threats such as phishing, ransomware, and malware affecting millions of users worldwide. Cybersecurity is no longer only a concern for large companies; everyday users can also become victims of identity theft, scams, and data breaches.

Many cyber incidents occur because of simple mistakes, such as clicking suspicious links, using weak passwords, or sharing sensitive information without caution. By increasing cybersecurity awareness and understanding common threats, people can make safer decisions and better protect themselves in the digital world.
